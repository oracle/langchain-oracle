The evolution of cinema has been marked by the transition from silent films to the "talkies," and eventually to the blockbuster era. Modern filmmaking relies heavily on high-frame-rate cinematography and CGI. A pivotal moment in sci-fi history was the introduction of "The Matrix," which utilized 'bullet time' effects. Contemporary audiences are now shifting toward streaming platforms, though the IMAX experience remains the gold standard for visual immersion in action-heavy spectacles like "Oppenheimer" or "Dune."
